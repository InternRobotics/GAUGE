01What is GAUGE

A diagnostic instrument for physical fidelity.

01

Measurement-grounded

Sixteen infrared cameras capture motion at 180 Hz. Repeated real-world trials provide millimeter-level trajectories, calibrated metadata, and uncertainty estimates.

02

Cross-regime

One benchmark spans rigid bodies, flexible cables, textiles, and volumetric soft bodies—from collision and friction to self-contact and large deformation.

03

Diagnostic evaluation

Trajectory error, equation form, physical parameters, and temporal stability are assessed separately so a plausible video cannot hide incorrect dynamics.

  1. 01Real experiment
  2. 02Calibrated metadata + repeated trajectories
  3. 03Engine / world-model evaluation
  4. 04Failure diagnosis

04Two Evaluation Tracks

One real-world foundation. Two diagnostic lenses.

Simulation-engine track

How accurately does a numerical simulator reproduce measured outcomes?

  1. 01ReconstructMatch real initial states, geometry, and calibrated parameters.
  2. 02SimulateRun Isaac Sim, Genesis, and Newton on 14 task families.
  3. 03CompareMeasure generalized trajectory error with RMSE, DTW, and task-specific metrics.

Video world-model track

Does generated motion obey the right laws at the right physical scale?

  1. 01ConditionProvide a shared first frame and the verified task prompt.
  2. 02GenerateEvaluate six image-to-video models on five rigid-body tasks.
  3. 03DiagnoseRecover SAM3 trajectories and test law form, parameters, and timing.

06Findings

Physical fidelity is mechanism-specific.

  1. 01

    No physics engine is uniformly faithful across regimes.

  2. 02

    Dynamic contact, rapid cloth motion, and volumetric deformation remain the largest sim-to-real gaps.

  3. 03

    Video world models often recover equation form while missing physical scale and timing.

Slope Slider

Best law form ≠ best acceleration

On wood, the lowest QFI is 13.61, while the closest generated acceleration is 2.06 m/s² against a 2.58 m/s² real baseline.

QFI: lower is better · acceleration: closer to baseline
Pendulum

A clean sinusoid can run at the wrong clock

Two outputs reach R² = 0.99, yet their periods are 1.93 s and 1.90 s versus the 1.06 s real period.

R²: higher is better · period: closer to baseline
Bouncing Ball

Free-fall shape without gravitational scale

The lowest reported QFI is 12.50, but its fitted acceleration is only 0.088 m/s² versus the 9.81 m/s² baseline.

QFI: lower is better · acceleration: closer to baseline
